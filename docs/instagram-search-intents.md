# Instagram search intents and content decisions

INS-154 review, 17 October 2026. This maps reader questions to existing pages; it is not a ranking report. Ordinary web searches and direct page reads informed the competitor observations below. Search position, traffic, query volume and competitors' performance were not measured. The owner's report of zero recent Google impressions remains the current recovery context; older Search Console evidence is historical. Neither these edits nor passing deployment checks establish recovery.

## Official export guidance: what was verified

[Meta's Accounts Center announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/10/manage-your-information-across-apps/) states that Download Your Information and Access Your Information moved into Accounts Center, accessible through Settings, and that Instagram and Facebook information can be requested together or separately. It was published in October 2023; it supports the location and account-selection context, not every current button label.

The current [Instagram information export help page](https://help.instagram.com/181231772500920) was attempted during this review but returned HTTP 429; the Facebook-hosted help mirror required login or blocked access. Therefore, exact October 2026 menus could not be independently verified against that official page. Competitor screenshots and wording are not treated as official confirmation. The guide retains qualified export/download wording, notes variation by version and language, and links directly to official help for changed menus. It does not promise preparation time, a fixed default date range or that every optional category exists for every account.

## Product evidence used for the copy

- `src/components/account-list.tsx`: the Following list supports Search accounts and displays usable recorded dates, with Date unavailable as the fallback.
- `src/lib/instagram/`: recognized JSON/HTML relationship records and split inputs; optional connection categories keep missing and explicitly empty evidence distinct. `connections.ts` separates pending requests from recent-request extras.
- `src/lib/analysis/relationships.ts`: mutuals are the intersection; fans are Followers minus Following; not-following-back is Following minus Followers. The guide's fictional 3-follower/4-following example yields 2 mutuals, 1 fan and 2 not-following-back accounts.
- Existing tool copy and product specification: source dates do not establish historical totals; a changed snapshot does not establish causality; archives stay local; public-photo lookup is a separate network-backed feature with conditional availability.

Only synthetic usernames and examples were used. No personal export or production analytics was inspected for these content changes. Actual Google indexing and impression recovery require later owner-visible Search Console evidence; the intent map is an editorial decision, not a claim that overlap caused the decline.
